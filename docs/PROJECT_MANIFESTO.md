# BrinMetal -- Project Manifesto

## Vision

Build the most authoritative digital presence for a metal fabrication company in Israel. Not a website -- an engineered experience that communicates precision, power, and 20+ years of mastery.

## Mission

Position BrinMetal as the premium choice for structural metal fabrication, architectural metalwork, and custom engineering solutions. Attract high-value clients -- construction companies, architects, and private homeowners who demand excellence.

## Who We Are

BrinMetal is a metal fabrication company based in the Netanya area, central Israel. Founded and led by Andrey -- an engineer, master fabricator, workshop leader, entrepreneur, and metal architect with over 20 years of hands-on experience.

This is not a small welding shop. This is an engineering operation.

## What We Do

- Heavy metal structures
- Structural reinforcement
- Gates and entry systems
- Fences and perimeter solutions
- Custom staircases
- Architectural metal fabrication
- Emergency metal works
- Custom one-off engineering solutions
- Complex welding projects

## Who We Serve

- Private homeowners seeking premium metalwork
- Construction companies requiring structural fabrication
- Architects and designers needing custom solutions
- Returning clients who trust our quality
- Projects requiring engineering-grade precision

## Positioning

BrinMetal is NOT a generic welding service. The brand stands alongside precision engineering firms, architectural studios, and industrial manufacturers. Think Porsche precision. Caterpillar power. Mercedes AMG industrial capability.

## Core Values

### Engineering First
Every project begins with engineering. Measurements, calculations, structural integrity -- before the first spark.

### Precision Over Speed
We do not rush. We engineer. Every weld, every cut, every assembly meets exacting standards.

### Trust Through Results
20+ years of completed projects speak louder than promises. Our work stands -- literally.

### Innovation Within Tradition
Modern techniques and materials combined with time-tested fabrication methods.

## Digital Experience Principles

1. **Authority** -- The website must immediately communicate expertise and capability
2. **Trust** -- Every element builds confidence in the company's ability to deliver
3. **Premium** -- Visual quality matches the quality of the physical work
4. **Clarity** -- Services, capabilities, and contact paths are immediately clear
5. **Scale** -- The digital platform must grow with the business
